Theoretical Grounding
Communication Theory Foundations
Three theories of deception and philosophical accounts of lying define the two axes of the taxonomy. Watch each one light up the part of the matrix it defines.
IDT
Interpersonal Deception Theory
Deception is an interactive process: deceiver and target keep adapting to each other. Each utterance serves a social goal.
→ The four social goal columns
TDT
Truth-Default Theory
People assume others tell the truth by default. Deceivers exploit this trust, which separates cooperative from adversarial goals.
→ The Cooperative to Adversarial axis
IMT2
Information Manipulation Theory 2
Speakers manipulate how much, how truthful, how relevant, and how clear their information is. This yields Transparent, Framing, and Counterfactual strategies.
→ Rows A, B, and D
Philosophy
Lying and Bullshitting
Frankfurt's account of bullshitting (indifference to truth) and Carson's account of lying separate careless speech from outright falsehood.
→ Row C, Careless-to-truth
