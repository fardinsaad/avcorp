Motivation
Why Detecting Deception Needs Theory of Mind
People trick AI models, and now AI agents push other agents to break rules. To detect deception, an investigator needs Theory of Mind: the ability to work out what others believe and want. AVCORP limits that reasoning to the depth people use.
